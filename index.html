About Karavaan

Karavaan is IISER Pune's annual festival, run entirely by students across every corner of campus — lecture halls, sports grounds, the auditorium and the Cricket Ground.

Over nine days, it moves from quizzes and quick sport to E-Sports, a talent night, Prom, and finally two Main Stage evenings of music, dance, comedy and a concert. The full day-by-day breakdown is below — worth a bookmark if you're planning your festival week.

Six sessions worth building your week around — the rest of the schedule is below.

26 January · Tuesday

Sports Showcase

Grounds & Indoor Sports Complex

A full day of cricket, football, frisbee, volleyball and tennis outdoors, plus basketball, table tennis, chess, badminton, carrom and a cubing competition indoors.

IISER's Got Latent

CV Raman

The open talent stage of the festival, run with Aroha, Navarasa, Naqab and the Hindi Club alongside the Karavaan Team.

All LHC rooms

Good to know

When does Karavaan run?

This academic year its planned for 9 days, from Saturday 23 January to Sunday 31 January 2027, across the IISER Pune campus.

Can I attend whole 9 day event?

No — Outsiders are only allowed for the IISER's Got Latent & two Main Stage nights at the Cricket Ground (30 & 31 January).

I'm from another college — can I take part?

Yes, several events welcome external college participants. Check the organiser listed for each event on the schedule for how to register.

Is the schedule final?

It's the current planned schedule and may see small changes closer to the date, especially around timings. This page will be kept up to date.
